Date: 2026-10-25 (America/Vancouver).

## Controls

- **Playback speed** (`components/playback-controls/TempoControl`): a compact `100%` trigger opens a slider (25–200%,
  steps of 5), a number field, presets 50/75/100/125/150% and Reset. The slider previews while it moves; a change is
  applied on release, on Enter or blur, or 300 ms after keyboard steps settle, so a held arrow key is one change.
  Copy says it changes speed only; pitch and written tempo markings are unchanged. Effective BPM is not shown: there
  is no reliable per-location beat-unit/BPM source yet, and the design forbids implying one fixed BPM.
- **Play repeats** (`PlayRepeatsToggle`): written repeats, voltas and jumps on or off, with the desktop meaning (off
  plays each written measure once). It is labelled as repeat structure, not looping; there is no loop feature.
- Commands: `playback.tempo` (presets), `playback.tempo.slower/faster` (±5%), `playback.tempo.reset` and
  `playback.playRepeats`, all announced. The Playback menu gains a Speed submenu and Play Repeats.
- Preferences (`lib/playback/playback-options-preferences.ts`) are local (`ots-playback-tempo`,
  `ots-playback-repeats`), shared by the editor and the embedded player. They never touch notation, undo or saved
  files; the browser test checks the undo index after a reload at 50%.

## Wiring

`usePlaybackOptionsSync` asks each loaded score for the `playbackOptions` capability (not a version) and applies the
preferences through the transport's Phase 3 position-preserving transaction (`setPlaybackOptions`). Unsupported
engines disable the controls with an explanation.

New in the transaction: a failed native change returns `status: 'failed'` with what the engine actually holds. The
transport resumes at the same musical place under those options, and the controls revert to them, so they never
claim an option that is not sounding.

The player runs the same sync inside `usePlaybackOptionsRefresh`, and its timeline/positions/seek range follow the
accepted revision.

## Transport redesign (`components/shell/Transport.tsx`)

Wide layout: `[Play/Pause] [Stop] position / duration [Selection] [Speed] [Play repeats] [Metronome] [Follow]
[Settings ▾]`. The Settings popover holds Count-in and Load SoundFont. Below `md`, only Play, Stop and the position
stay in the bar; Selection, Speed, Repeats, Metronome and Follow move into the Settings popover. The player bar gains
the same Speed and Repeats controls, in its own look, in the wide bar and the narrow "More" menu.

- **Position:** published by `useTransportTimePublisher` into a small store at most 4×/s from the transport's
  position subscription. The editor root does not re-render for it. The editor's duration is now re-read once each
  play is live, so an edit that changed the length no longer leaves the label stale.
- **Preparation feedback**, from its own `audioPreparing` source (separate from export busy and layout "Finalizing
  layout…"): Play acknowledges the press at once (spinner, `aria-busy`). "Preparing audio…" appears only after
  250 ms, and Stop becomes Cancel while preparing.
